Wheels: Gullideckel, the standard 15″ “manhole cover” alloy fitted to most production 190E models. A future update could add Bundt or Evolution wheel options to switch between.

Base model: “1982 Mercedes W201” by Dave Love (Tyler_Dave), licensed under CC BY 4.0, provided the core chassis geometry. Additional modeling, exterior surfacing, interior, materials, lighting, paint, and the interactive glTF viewer (rotate, zoom, live color swap) are original work by Hang Yuan.
