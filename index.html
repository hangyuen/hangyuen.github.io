I'm Hang /hʌŋ/, an interdisciplinary designer and creative technologist based in Evansville, IN — or anywhere.

I mainly work with digital mediums to create interactive experiences and visuals that resonate with people.

Currently, I teach digital media and game design courses as an Assistant Professor at the University of Southern Indiana.

Voyage

Hang is originally from Shenzhen, right by Hong Kong, where it's summer nearly all year and the ocean is always close by.

He moved to the United States to study at the University of Nebraska–Lincoln, earning a BFA and BA. Afterward, he worked as a game artist, digital designer, and in IT across a few different cities, before heading to Iowa State University, where he earned an MFA in Integrated Visual Arts, emphasis in Digital Media, and an MS in Human-Computer Interaction.

That path, art and creative technology, design and computer science, shaped his interdisciplinary approach today.

Bon voyage.
